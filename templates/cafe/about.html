Our story

Warm cups, honest food,
an easy place to be

Wow Weffles is a small café in Dulera with a simple belief: good coffee, fresh chai and crisp waffles shouldn’t come with a fuss.

How it began

A neighbourhood corner, reimagined

We wanted a space that felt calm and modern but never cold: earthy browns and olive greens, warm light, and a menu you can trust. Somewhere a student can nurse a chai over notes, an office team can grab a quick coffee, and a family can share waffles on a Sunday.

So we built exactly that. Everything is brewed and made to order, priced fairly, and served with a smile. That’s the whole idea.
