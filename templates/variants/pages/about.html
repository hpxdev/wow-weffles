Our story

Warm cups,
honest food.

Wow Weffles is a small café in Dulera with a simple belief: good coffee, fresh chai and crisp waffles should not come with a fuss.

A neighbourhood corner, reimagined

We wanted a space that felt calm and modern but never cold: earthy browns and olive greens, warm light, and a menu you can trust. Somewhere a student can nurse a chai, an office team can grab a quick coffee, and a family can share waffles on a Sunday.

Everything is brewed and made to order, priced fairly, and served with a smile. That is the whole idea.

Fresh & honest

Real ingredients, made when you order. No shortcuts on the things that matter.

For everyone

Students, families, couples and office folks. Our space and prices welcome all of Dulera.

Calm & clean

A modern, minimal room that is easy to settle into for hours.
